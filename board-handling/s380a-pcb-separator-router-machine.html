Core Capabilities & Features

Full Vision System & Simplified Programming

Equipped with a "Full vision curve splitter" and CCD visual programming, allowing technicians to "draw straight lines and arcs on the picture" for programming, significantly reducing editing and debugging time. Supports diverse shapes including straight lines, arcs, circles, L-shaped, U-shaped, stamp-hole, and V-slot models.

High-Speed Spindle & Precision

Utilizes a high-speed spindle (20,000 to 60,000 rpm) ensuring low cutting stress and high precision for PCB boards of any shape. Achieves machine repetition accuracy of ±0.01mm and cutting accuracy of ±0.02mm.

Dual Y-axis Table Work (Double Table)

Features two independent Y-axes (Y1 and Y2) enabling simultaneous split board cutting and PCB board placement, or cutting two different products concurrently, significantly enhancing operational efficiency.

Automated Tool Management

Includes Automatic Tool Change Function (Q380A configuration) to save downtime and reduce operator stress, and Broken Tool Detection to monitor tool state in real-time, preventing faulty products.

Automatic Tool Height Correction

Independently developed function to automatically adjust the Z-axis height of the cutting tool after a tool change, ensuring consistent Z-axis tool drop position and preventing damage to PCBs or components.

Safety & Reliability

Equipped with a safety grating and an emergency button. Powered by Panasonic A6 series AC servo motors for X, Y1, Z, and Y2 axes, ensuring safe and stable operation. Software compatible with Windows 7 and 10.
