The High Cost of "Good Enough" Depaneling

As managers in high-volume EMS facilities, you recognize these challenges. Traditional methods are a direct risk to your reputation and your bottom line, especially with today's thin, densely populated boards.

High Defect Rates

Manual 'hand-breaks' and older V-cut machines introduce significant mechanical stress, leading to micro-cracks in solder joints and component damage, resulting in costly field failures.

Labor Costs & Scalability

You face rising labor costs and simply cannot scale manual, operator-dependent processes to meet demand. This creates a critical production bottleneck and limits your factory's growth.

Failing Quality Audits

The inability to process complex, non-rectangular PCBs and the risk of hidden defects puts you under constant pressure from end-clients (e.g., Automotive, Medical) for 100% quality.

The S380A: Precision, Automation, & Throughput

The S380A is a fully automated, vision-guided routing system. It's designed to solve the three core challenges of modern depaneling. Explore its value by clicking the tabs below.

FAB 1: From "High Risk" to "Zero Stress"

(F) Feature: High-speed 60,000 RPM spindle and Panasonic A6 servo drives, delivering cutting accuracy of ±0.02mm and repeat accuracy of ±0.01mm.

(A) Advantage: This enables 'stress-free' milling of any shape—arcs, circles, L-shapes, and stamp-holes. The routing bit cuts the board cleanly, unlike the 'cracking' force of a V-cut blade, even 0.5mm from components.

(B) Benefit: This directly reduces your PCB defect rate, virtually eliminating scrap cost from depaneling. You can confidently process complex, non-rectangular boards for high-value clients and pass the strictest quality audits.

FAB 2: From "Complex" to "Automated"

(F) Feature: A CCD "whole board" visual programming system with automatic tool height correction and broken tool detection.

(A) Advantage: This 'visual programming' subverts traditional, complex G-code. An operator can simply 'draw' the cut path on the screen. Setup for new jobs is reduced from hours to minutes, with minimal training required.

(B) Benefit: This slashes your labor costs and dependency on highly-skilled technicians. It provides true production line flexibility, allowing you to quickly switch between high-mix, low-volume models, making those jobs more profitable.

FAB 3: From "Idle" to "Continuous Flow"

(F) Feature: A dual Y-axis table configuration (Y1 and Y2).

(A) Advantage: This design allows for continuous production. The machine executes cutting on Table Y1 *while* the operator simultaneously unloads/loads Table Y2. The spindle *never* sits idle.

(B) Benefit: This effectively doubles your production throughput compared to a single-table machine. It's designed for 24/7 operation, improving your overall order delivery efficiency and maximizing your factory's output.
