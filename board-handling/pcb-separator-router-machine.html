Core Capabilities

🖥️

Full Vision System

CCD whole-board visual programming allows technicians to simply "draw" cutting paths on an image, drastically reducing programming and debugging time for any shape.

⚡

High-Speed Spindle

With a range of 20,000 to 60,000 RPM, the spindle ensures low cutting stress and high precision for any PCB shape, minimizing component damage.

🔄

Dual Y-axis Table

Enables simultaneous cutting and board placement, or cutting two different products concurrently, significantly boosting operational efficiency.

🛠️

Automated Tool Management

Features automatic tool change when a tool's service life is reached and broken tool detection to ensure consistent quality and maximum uptime.

📏

Auto Tool Height Correction

Automatically adjusts the Z-axis height after a tool change, compensating for variations and preventing damage to the PCB or components.

🛡️

Advanced Safety Features

Integrated safety grating and an emergency stop button ensure operator safety during all phases of machine operation.

About Southern Machinery

Southern Machinery develops intelligent, automatic production equipment designed for the continuous cutting and depaneling of Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs). Our machines, like the S380A, are engineered for smart EMS (Electronic Manufacturing Services) factories, offering high-precision, efficient, and user-friendly solutions. With a global reach and a commitment to customer assistance, we provide robust support services and online resources to ensure our clients achieve maximum productivity and quality in their electronics manufacturing processes.
