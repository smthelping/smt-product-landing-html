Precision Pick-and-Place with Custom Nozzles

Our nozzles are the cornerstone of precision assembly. We address common pain points like clogging and sticking with innovative designs and superior materials. From standard chips to the most challenging odd-form components, we provide a custom-fit solution that enhances pick-up rates to 99.99% and ensures flawless placement.

  • ✓ Optimized for odd-form components, jumper wires, and special connectors.
  • ✓ Large-passage and self-cleaning designs to eliminate downtime.
  • ✓ Anti-static properties to protect your most sensitive components.

Secure Handling with Mechanical Grippers

When standard vacuum nozzles aren't enough, our custom grippers step in. Engineered for secure handling of irregularly shaped or delicate components, our grippers use a mechanical fixed-and-swing-arm design. This ensures reliable control and placement accuracy for components that are otherwise difficult to automate.

  • ✓ Wide gripping range from 0.8mm to 5.2mm for maximum versatility.
  • ✓ Ideal for THT components, large connectors, and shields.
  • ✓ Robust construction for long-term reliability in high-volume production.

Built with Premium Materials for Peak Performance

The quality of your final product depends on the quality of your tools. We use only the best materials to ensure our nozzles and grippers deliver unmatched durability, wear resistance, and precision. This commitment to quality means fewer replacements, less maintenance, and more consistent output.

  • ✓ Diamond Steel: Combines strength with oxidation resistance for maximum lifespan.
  • ✓ Ceramic: Provides superior hardness and precision for nozzle tips, preventing wear.
  • ✓ Stainless Steel: Ensures robust, corrosion-resistant bodies for all our tools.
