Shaping the Future of Electronics Manufacturing since 2011.

Established in the bustling tech hub of Shenzhen in 2011, Southern Machinery stands as a testament to innovation, quality, and efficiency in the electronics manufacturing industry. With a keen focus on the Surface Mount Technology (SMT) and Through-Hole Technology (THT) sectors, our company specializes in the design and manufacture of state-of-the-art machinery and equipment aimed at revolutionizing PCB assembly automation processes.

At Southern Machinery, we understand the intricacies of electronic component placement and soldering. Our machines are crafted to cater to the intricate needs of modern electronics production, ensuring precision, durability, and ease of use. Whether you require high-speed assembly or the delicate precision for small batch production, our product lineup is equipped to handle your demands, providing low-cost, high-efficiency solutions that are second to none.

Our commitment to enhancing assembly automation extends beyond our machinery. We are pioneers in incorporating the rich manufacturing experience of Shenzhen—a city renowned for its electronics production prowess—into our operations, products, and services. By doing so, we not only offer top-tier equipment but also a wealth of knowledge and best practices to our clients worldwide.

Customized Feeder & Nozzle Solutions

Engineered for precision, compatibility, and performance.

SAF1001 Axial Tape Feeder

Integrates with SMT machines for automated insertion, supporting non-standard equipment and precise U-shaping for components like resistors and diodes.

SRF1001 Radial Tape Feeder

Automates feeding, shaping, and cutting of radial components. Customizable for odd-form parts with options for tape cutting and bending.

STF1003 Tube Feeder

Automates feeding of tube-mounted components like DIPs and transistors. Saves labor and enhances production stability.

SBF1001 Bowl Feeder

Automates bulk component feeding, sorting, and screening. Enables robotic integration for fully automated production lines.

SLF1001 Label Feeder

Automates placement of QR labels and FPC reinforcement sheets for MES integration, saving labor and costs.

Custom & Gripper Nozzles

High-precision nozzles for odd-form components, THT parts, and special applications, featuring enhanced durability and wear resistance.
