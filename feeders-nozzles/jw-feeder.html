Technical Brief

Analysis: A Critical Inefficiency in SMT/THT Assembly

Manual jumper wire insertion introduces unacceptable variables into a controlled process, directly impacting OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) and per-unit profitability.

The reliance on manual labor for this task creates a cascading effect: inconsistent speeds cause downstream bottlenecks, variable placement quality leads to higher rework rates, and imprecise material handling results in significant waste. This memo outlines a definitive, engineered solution.

The Solution: The SM-JWF-01 Automated Jumper Wire Feeder

The Southern Machinery Automated Jumper Wire Feeder is designed to replace this manual process with a stable, high-speed, and precise system. The objective is to deliver a measurable ROI through three core benefits:

  • Maximize Throughput: Eliminate the human-speed limitation and accelerate your entire line.
  • Reduce Operational Costs: Achieve verifiable savings by eliminating material waste and reducing direct labor costs.
  • Guarantee Process Consistency: Ensure every jumper wire is cut, formed, and placed with machine precision, improving final product quality.
