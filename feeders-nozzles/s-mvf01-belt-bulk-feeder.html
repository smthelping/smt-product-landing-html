The feeding principle

From bulk supply to repeatable presentation.

The S-MVF01 combines motor-driven belt transport with direct vibration at the pick area. The result is a compact route for physically distinguishable bulk parts that need to reach an SMT mounter in a stable orientation.

01

Load in bulk

Loose components enter the feed area without requiring tape or tray packaging.

02

Select on the belt

The selection track separates parts with distinguishable physical characteristics.

03

Return non-selected parts

NG or non-selected material returns by belt for the next selection cycle.

Application fit

Best when component handling needs more flexibility than a dedicated bowl.

S-MVF01 is intended for suitable bulk components such as connectors, buzzers, network ports, terminals and square capacitors. It should be selected after a physical review of the part, pins and orientation needs.

Bring these to the application review

  • Component photos, drawings and a sample quantity
  • Overall dimensions, pin geometry and any marking/orientation requirement
  • Placement machine brand, model and available interface space
  • Target cycle time and expected production volume

Important fit limits

  • Parts must have distinguishable physical characteristics for selection
  • Components with easily deformed pins are not recommended
  • Parts with a length-to-height ratio below 1:1.2 are not recommended
  • Final tooling and controls are confirmed by the Southern Machinery engineering team
