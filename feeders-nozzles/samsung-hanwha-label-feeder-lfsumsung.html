Technical evaluation FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering

Which Samsung machines does the LFSUMSUNG fit?

The LFSUMSUNG series is designed for Samsung SM320, SM321, SM411, SM431, SM471, SM481 and SM482 (SME series) pick-and-place machines.

What is the difference between LFSUMSUNG-60 and LFSUMSUNG-100?

The two models cover different label size and speed ranges of the same family; the -100 supports a larger label configuration. Final model selection is confirmed against your label dimensions.

How fast can it feed labels?

Fully automatic peeling and feeding at 1 to 100 pieces per minute, with speed adjustable to your line rhythm.

What label sizes are supported?

Labels up to 100 mm wide are supported on the reference configuration; custom label widths and feeding setups are available on request.

How is feeding accuracy maintained?

The unit documents ±0.2 mm feeding accuracy with stable feed-out, so the placement head picks every label from the same position.
