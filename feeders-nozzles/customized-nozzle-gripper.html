The "Odd" Challenge

Why automation fails here

Odd Form Components (connectors, relays, shields) represent the final frontier of SMT automation. While standard chips are placed at 50,000 CPH, these irregular parts cause disproportionate downtime due to center-of-gravity offsets and lack of flat suction surfaces.

Material Engineering

Balancing ESD, Weight & Wear

We don't just use steel. The choice of material dictates the lifespan of the tool and the safety of the component. PEEK (Ceramic Filled) is our standard for ESD safety and low inertia, while Tool Steel is reserved for high-wear gripper jaws.

PEEK (ESD Safe) Best for: Nozzle Tips, Delicate Grippers
Stainless Steel (304) Best for: Rigid Body Structure, Cleanroom
Aluminum (7075) Best for: Moving Parts (Low Inertia)
