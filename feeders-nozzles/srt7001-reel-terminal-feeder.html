How SRT7001 Works in Your THT Flow

1. Reel Loading & Tape Guidance

The operator mounts the terminal reel on the feeder shaft, routes the tape through the guide path, and positions it at the pick point defined in the 3D model.[web:15][web:93]

  • Reel‑type carrier for terminals ensures consistent pitch and orientation.
  • Guides and clamps keep the tape centered and flat at the pick location.[web:64][web:79]
  • Mechanism designed to minimize tape skew and protect terminal geometry.[web:66][web:83]

2. Indexed Terminal Presentation

An internal drive indexes the tape by one terminal pitch per cycle, presenting each terminal at the same mechanical reference point relative to your pick‑and‑place or odd‑form insertion head.[web:4][web:86]

  • Pitch controlled to match the terminal spacing defined by the reel.[web:86]
  • Sensors detect presence/empty and protect against mis‑picks and jams.[web:64][web:69]

3. Pick, Insert, and Wave Solder

With customized nozzles or grippers, your odd‑form head picks terminals directly from the SRT7001 pick point and inserts them into THT holes on the PCB before wave soldering.[web:49][web:54][file:47]

  • Mechanical interfaces designed so the pick point matches your head X/Y/Z range.[web:3][web:93]
  • Suitable for integration with JUKI and other platforms via dedicated brackets and I/O mapping.[web:3][web:87]
  • After insertion, boards flow through wave solder as part of a complete SMT + THT line.[file:47]
