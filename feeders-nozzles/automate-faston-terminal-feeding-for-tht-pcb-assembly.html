Key Features

SRT7001 is engineered to turn terminal reels into a stable, machine-ready supply for terminal insertion, supporting high throughput and repeatable pick accuracy on SMT/THT hybrid lines.

RR

Reel-to-Reel Feeding System

Continuous reel feeding reduces changeover downtime and keeps terminal presentation consistent across long production runs.

UC

Universal Compatibility

Compatible with multiple SMT equipment brands, supporting Panasonic NPM/CM, JUKI RS/RX, and YAMAHA YS series through custom brackets and I/O mapping.

PX

Precision Feeding Mechanism

Stable indexing and guided tape path deliver ±0.3mm feeding accuracy to improve terminal insertion quality and reduce mis-picks.

NZ

Customizable Nozzle Options

Custom nozzles and grippers adapt to different terminal sizes and geometries, enabling damage-free pick and consistent insertion.
