Feeder Details & Operation

Core Function & Mechanism

The primary function of a stick feeder is to reliably deliver electronic components packaged in tubes (sticks) to a repeatable pick point for the SMT placement head.

Packaging:

Components suitable for this feeder are typically multi-pin parts, such as DIP (Dual Inline Package) parts, SOICs, PLCCs, or other components that are not available in tape or tray formats.

Vibratory Advance:

The system uses a vibrating feeder to ensure that components contained within the tube (stick) continuously move forward and enter the required absorption position for the placement head. This vibration mechanism or a belt drive shuttles the components one at a time to the pickup point.

Pick Presentation:

The vibration or mechanical drive indexes the component to the fixed pickup coordinates, allowing the machine's nozzle to retrieve the part.

Operation & Structure (S-JVF02 Example)

The S-JVF02 model, compatible with the JUKI RS-1, illustrates the typical structure and input method of this technology.

Component Input:

The components are packaged in tubes (sticks). The feeder is designed to handle components ranging in size from 3x3mm up to 30x10mm.

Loading the Tube:

When loading the feeder, the component tube must be positioned and fixed carefully:

  • The tube end should be placed above the support frame and secured by a locating ring.
  • The middle section of the tube is held under a leaf spring and kept under pressure.
  • The front end of the tube is fixed by a locating piece.
  • The top face of the tube, over the nozzle picking area, must be cut off to expose the components for the placement head.
Channels and Power:

The S-JVF02 model has 4 input channels and operates using 24V DC power.

Context within JUKI Systems

The JUKI RS-1 is a pick-and-place machine. JUKI's machines (including the KE, FX, and RS-1/RS-1R series) use intelligent feeders that offer optimization capabilities via systems like IFS/NM.

Compared to tape feeders (the most common SMT feeder type), stick feeders are less common, often providing a solution for legacy components or those with less standardized packaging.

Analogy

"If a high-speed tape feeder is like a machine gun belt, automatically peeling back layers to reveal ammunition in rapid sequence, a vibration stick feeder is more like a coin dispenser attached to a soda machine. The components (coins) are pre-loaded in an organized column (the tube/stick), and vibration is applied to ensure gravity and friction continuously push the next item to the exact final output slot, ready for immediate robotic pickup."
