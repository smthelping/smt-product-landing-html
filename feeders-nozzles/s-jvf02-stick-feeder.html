Overview

Designed for EMS production realities

In EMS environments, tube/stick components can create interruptions due to reloading, handling, and feeding instability. S-JVF02 targets stable feeding behavior using a vibration-based mechanism and provides 4 input channels to reduce reload frequency.

Typical Objectives

Operational outcomes

  • Reduce line interruptions Minimize stop-and-reload events associated with tube components.
  • Support high-mix workflows Shorter recovery and smoother operation during frequent changeovers.
  • Lower manual handling risk Less repeated handling during feeding and reloading cycles.
