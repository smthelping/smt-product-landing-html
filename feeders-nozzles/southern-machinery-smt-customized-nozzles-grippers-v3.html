Customized SMT Nozzles

Our nozzles are the cornerstone of precision assembly. We address common pain points like clogging and sticking with innovative designs and superior materials. From standard chips to the most challenging odd-form components, we provide a custom-fit solution that enhances pick-up rates to 99.99% and ensures flawless placement.

  • ✓ High-grade ceramic and diamond steel construction.
  • ✓ Anti-static properties to protect sensitive components.
  • ✓ Designs for Jumper Wires, special connectors, and more.

Customized Grippers

When standard vacuum nozzles aren't enough, our custom grippers step in. Engineered for secure handling of irregularly shaped or delicate components, our grippers use a mechanical fixed-and-swing-arm design. This ensures reliable control and placement accuracy for components that are otherwise difficult to automate.

  • ✓ Wide gripping range from 0.8mm to 5.2mm.
  • ✓ Ergonomic design for reduced operator fatigue in manual applications.
  • ✓ Versatile enough for THT components, connectors, and shields.

Your Trusted Partner for SMT & THT Solutions

Established in the bustling tech hub of Shenzhen in 2011, Southern Machinery stands as a testament to innovation, quality, and efficiency in the electronics manufacturing industry. Our commitment to enhancing assembly automation extends beyond our machinery. We are pioneers in incorporating the rich manufacturing experience of Shenzhen—a city renowned for its electronics production prowess—into our operations, products, and services. By doing so, we not only offer top-tier equipment but also a wealth of knowledge and best practices to our clients worldwide.
