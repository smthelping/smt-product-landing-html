About Southern Machinery

Shenzhen Southern Machinery Sales and Service Co., Ltd. designs and manufactures SMT and THT equipment for smart EMS factories: odd-form insertion machines, tape / tube / tray / bowl feeders, custom nozzles and grippers, board handling and turnkey lines.

Feeders are engineered against the mounter they will run on. Where a standard format does not fit the component, the tooling is designed to the component instead.

Tell us the component and the mounter

Send the component drawing or a photo and the mounter model. We will confirm whether a standard feeder covers it or whether the tooling has to be designed.
