Discover the S7900

Automate Your Most Challenging Insertion Tasks

The **S7900 Odd Form Series Insertion Machine** is engineered to master the complexities of modern PCB assembly. It simulates and replaces laborious manual operations, delivering unparalleled precision and efficiency when handling non-standard, "odd-form" electronic components.

Designed with advanced multi-axis motion, a sophisticated vision system, and intuitive software, the S7900 ensures flawless placement, boosts throughput, and significantly enhances your production line's capabilities.

Simplified Operation: From Startup to Production

  1. Easy Startup: Connect power and air, turn on the main switch and computer, launch the software, and perform a quick machine zeroing.
  2. Program Creation: Effortlessly create new programs by defining part data, PCB parameters, MARK points, and teaching block/hole data.
  3. Automated Learning: Utilize the automatic learning feature to quickly define insertion data, ensuring efficient setup for new components.
  4. Flexible Array Editing: Choose between general editing for single PCBs or advanced array editing for panel form PCBs.
  5. Quick Nozzle Replacement: Tool-free nozzle/gripper replacement ensures minimal downtime and maximum versatility.
