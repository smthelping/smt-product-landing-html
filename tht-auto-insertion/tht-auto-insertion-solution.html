Our THT Product Line

Engineered for reliability and performance, our machines handle a wide range of components.

AI-6488 Jumper Wire Inserter

High-speed insertion of jumper wires to replace traditional manual processes. Enhances connectivity and structural integrity of PCBs.

  • Insertion Speed: 0.4s/piece
  • Supports various jumper spans
  • PC control with user-friendly interface

AI-6188 Radial Component Inserter

Versatile machine for inserting radial components like capacitors, LEDs, and transistors with high accuracy.

  • Insertion Speed: 0.5s/piece
  • Handles 20-100 component types
  • Precise servo-driven system

AI-6288 Axial Component Inserter

Reliably inserts axial components such as resistors and diodes, crucial for a wide range of electronic circuits.

  • Insertion Speed: 0.4s/piece
  • Handles 40-160 component types
  • Automatic PCB loading/unloading options
