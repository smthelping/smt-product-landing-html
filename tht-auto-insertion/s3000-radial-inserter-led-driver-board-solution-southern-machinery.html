The hidden cost of hand insertion

Three operators per shift, and the pace still does not hold

Radial capacitors are small, polarized and volume-hungry. On a 60,000-board month they turn a staffing question into a throughput and quality question.

16,154 insertions, three people

Every board carries 14 radial capacitors. Across a 1,154-board shift that is 16,154 hand placements — a workload that scales with headcount, not with your line speed.

Polarity errors are silent

A reversed electrolytic capacitor looks correct on the bench and fails only at electrical test. The defect is invisible at the station that created it.

Night shift quality drift

Turnover and seasonal staffing re-introduce exactly the placement errors the process was written to prevent, and they cluster on the shifts you can least supervise.

Hands between processes

Bare boards and finished panels move between the SMT section and wave soldering by hand, adding handling damage and WIP that nobody measures.

Traceability gaps

Manual insertion cannot document which component went into which board, when. Your EMS customers increasingly refuse to carry that risk.

Labour economics tighten

Wages rise and availability falls. Every manual insertion station quietly raises the cost per board, while your selling price does not move.

Three reasons this fits your numbers

Your panel fits with room to spare

A 180 × 120 mm board run 2-up is 360 × 120 mm. That sits inside the 50 × 50 – 400 × 300 mm envelope quoted on the current product page, and inside the wider 450 × 450 mm envelope in the datasheet PDF — so the fit holds whichever source your engineering team prefers. Board thickness of 1.6 mm sits inside the 0.79 – 2.36 mm range.

The shift's radial work fits in about 1.6 hours

1,154 boards per shift × 14 capacitors is 16,154 insertions. At the 10,000 CPH actual production speed — the figure Southern Machinery quotes rather than the peak — that is roughly 1.6 hours of machine time. On a 10-hour shift the radial step would occupy about 16% of capacity, leaving headroom for growth or additional board types.

Parts your maintenance team can already source

The machine runs on Universal-compatible wear parts per the current product page, with a 1-day spare lead time quoted in the service flow below, so you are not locked into one proprietary channel. The datasheet PDF states running cost differently — that conflict is flagged in the specification notes rather than averaged away.

Technical evaluation FAQ

Questions this board raises

Does my 180 × 120 mm board fit, and can it run 2-up?

Yes. The minimum is 50 × 50 mm and the maximum is 400 × 300 mm per the current product page (450 × 450 mm per the datasheet PDF). A 2-up panel of 360 × 120 mm fits inside both envelopes, and your 1.6 mm thickness sits inside the 0.79 – 2.36 mm range.

How much machine time does my shift actually need?

1,154 boards per shift × 14 capacitors = 16,154 insertions. At the 10,000 CPH actual production speed that is about 1.6 hours, or roughly 16% of a 10-hour shift. Confirm your shift length and we will restate the utilisation.

Which of my components can this machine insert — and which can't it?

The S3000 covers radial tape-package components: capacitors (electrolytic, ceramic, box, film), transistors, diodes, LEDs, connectors, tact switches, coils, potentiometers, lamps and fuses, with spans of 2.5 to 7.5 mm and a maximum body height of 20 mm. Your 14 radial capacitors are inside that range. Your 6 axial resistors and 4 terminals are not — those need separate machines, and we have not yet read their datasheets, so we are not quoting capacity for them.

Will I be locked into proprietary spare parts?

The current product page states 100% compatibility with Universal machine wear-and-tear parts. Be aware that the datasheet PDF quotes running cost as RMB 2,000/month/unit, while the product page quotes under $100/month — the two figures differ and may cover different cost scopes. We will confirm the scope with engineering before you rely on either number.

What utilities and floor space does it need?

220 V / 50 Hz single phase and compressed air at 0.6 – 0.8 MPa. A built-in air storage tank and a built-in 3 KVA regulator stabilise both. The machine is 1700 × 1500 × 1600 mm and weighs 880 kg.

How do I get a price and a delivery time?

Email info@smthelp.com or WhatsApp +86 136 0256 2576 with your board sizes, component mix and quantity — you have already given us most of it. Quotation and delivery schedule follow technical confirmation, typically the same working day. The two numbers that would let us finish the payback model are your loaded hourly rate and your current measured defect rate.
