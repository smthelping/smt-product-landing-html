Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered.

What is THT automatic assembly?

THT automatic assembly automates the insertion of leaded components into a PCB. Our machines handle this entire process, increasing production speed, accuracy, and efficiency while significantly reducing labor costs.

What are the benefits of using your machines?

Key benefits include drastically increased production speed, improved placement accuracy, reduced labor costs, enhanced overall product quality and reliability, and a shorter lead time for your projects.

Which industries benefit most?

Our solutions are ideal for a wide range of industries, including consumer electronics, automotive electronics, industrial automation, aerospace, telecommunications, and medical devices.

How does insertion force monitoring improve quality?

This feature ensures components are inserted with the correct force, preventing damage to the component or PCB. It detects issues like improper seating or bent leads, improving the overall reliability of the assembly.
