Technical evaluation FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering

What is the difference between selective soldering and wave soldering?

Wave soldering floods the whole bottom of the board with solder; selective soldering applies solder only to the joints you program, using a moving mini-wave or solder nozzle. That protects temperature-sensitive components and removes the need for fixtures on high-mix boards.

Which boards can the S-100B handle?

The S-100B is designed for high-mix, low-volume THT assembly — labs, prototyping, small production lines and boards that change frequently. Board size and joint program are confirmed during the sample trial.

Do I need nitrogen?

Yes — the S-100B uses N2 preheat to protect the board and reduce oxidation, and a nitrogen source is required as a consumable input.

What nozzle sizes are available?

Standard nozzles are 4 mm and 5 mm, made of alloy steel with silver plating; custom shapes are available for unusual joint geometries.

How is accuracy maintained?

The 3-axis robot uses X/Y stepper motors and a Z-axis servo with ball screw on high-quality linear guides, holding ±0.05 mm positioning and repeatability on a 100 kg stable base.
