From Concept to Component: Our Customization Journey

We've perfected a collaborative and transparent process to deliver precision-engineered SMT tools that solve your unique assembly challenges.

01

Consultation & Needs Analysis

Our journey begins with understanding your needs. We'll discuss your specific component, SMT machine, production goals, and any pain points you're experiencing, such as component damage, low pick-up rates, or misalignment.

02

Engineering & 3D Design

Using the information gathered, our expert engineers create a detailed 3D model and technical drawings of your custom nozzle or gripper. We focus on optimizing every detail, from material selection to the precise shape of the tip, for maximum performance.

03

Precision Manufacturing

Once you approve the design, we move to manufacturing. Using state-of-the-art CNC machinery and premium materials like diamond steel and ceramic, we bring the design to life with exacting tolerances and a flawless finish.

04

Quality Assurance & Delivery

Every custom part undergoes rigorous quality control checks to ensure it meets our high standards and your exact specifications. After passing inspection, your order is securely packaged and shipped globally, backed by our ongoing support for seamless integration.

Why Invest in a Custom Solution?

Standard parts lead to standard problems. Customization delivers targeted benefits that impact your bottom line.

Eliminate Errors

Custom-fit tools drastically reduce component shifting, misalignment, and damage, leading to higher first-pass yields.

Boost Throughput

By solving handling issues for odd-form parts, you can fully automate your line and increase overall production speed.

Reduce Waste

Fewer damaged components and incorrect placements mean less scrap material and lower operational costs.
