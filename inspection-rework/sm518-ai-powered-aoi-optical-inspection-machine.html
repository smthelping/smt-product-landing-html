05 — Defect Detection Gallery

What the SM518 Catches Before It Ships

Missing Component

Empty pad positions detected with OCV character verification.

Solder Bridge

Short-circuit algorithm detects channel bridges with linear interference filtering to reduce false positives.

Insufficient Solder

Colour analysis of solder joint curvature identifies low-solder fillets.

Tombstone / Standing

Chip component vertical lift detected by 3D colour imaging.

Polarity Reverse

Automatic polarity detection for diodes, LEDs, and polarised capacitors.

Position Offset

Sub-pixel accuracy measures component shift against placement coordinates.

What is the programming time for SM518 AOI?

What defects can the SM518 detect?

The SM518 detects missing components, wrong parts, offset, skew, tombstone, sideways, flip, broken parts, solder bridge, insufficient solder, solder balls, glue issues, and pin exposure problems.

What is the minimum component size?

At 15um/pixel resolution, the SM518 supports 01005 chip inspection and 0.3mm pitch IC inspection.
