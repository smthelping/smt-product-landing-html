Technical evaluation FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering

Which Yamaha machines do you support?

The nozzle list covers the Yamaha mounter range; send your machine model and nozzle code and we match the correct part.

How is the adsorption rate?

Yamaha nozzles target a 99.99% component adsorption rate, minimizing missed picks on the head.

How long do the nozzles last?

The reference service life is more than 24 months, with abrasion-resistant tips that will not turn worse easily.

Are the nozzles ESD safe?

Yes — nozzle materials are engineered to avoid static electricity build-up, protecting sensitive components.

Can you make custom Yamaha nozzles?

Yes — custom nozzles and grippers are designed around your special components, with 3D samples available for review.
