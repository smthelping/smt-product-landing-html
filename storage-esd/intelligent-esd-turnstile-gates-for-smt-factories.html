Simple, Secure, and Swift Operation

A seamless four-step process to ensure total ESD protection at every entry point.

1. Identification

Employee verifies identity via card, fingerprint, or facial scan.

2. ESD Testing

System simultaneously tests wrist strap and footwear on the dual footplate.

3. Verification

Instant Pass/Fail result is displayed with clear visual and audible indicators.

4. Access Granted

On 'Pass', the gate unlocks. On 'Fail', access is denied, ensuring EPA integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your top questions about our Intelligent ESD Access Control Systems, answered.

The system's primary goal is to prevent electrostatic discharge (ESD) damage by ensuring all personnel are properly grounded before entering an Electrostatic Protection Area (EPA). It acts as a gatekeeper, verifying anti-static gear is functional, which protects sensitive electronic components, enhances security, improves productivity, and ensures compliance with industry standards like ANSI/ESD S20.20.

Our systems are designed for high accuracy and reliability. They use a 100V test voltage to avoid false results caused by contamination on shoe soles. The testing process is extremely fast—checking both feet and a wrist strap in under 2 seconds—and the hardware features solid-state buttons for longevity. Annual calibration is recommended to maintain peak accuracy.

The system collects comprehensive data, including detailed test results (Pass/Fail, resistance values), personnel information (ID, name, department), and access logs (entry/exit times). This data is invaluable for creating paperless audit trails, monitoring compliance in real-time, generating automated reports for operational insights, and integrating with HR or ERP systems for streamlined workforce management.

Absolutely. The system is highly customizable and scalable. You can configure flexible test modes for different roles (e.g., test-free for management), set granular access permissions, and choose from various gate types. The networked architecture allows you to easily add more turnstiles and manage numerous entry points across one or multiple facilities from a central software platform as your operations grow.
