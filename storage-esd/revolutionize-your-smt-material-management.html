Key Features

Engineered for Peak Performance

Our system is packed with advanced technology to streamline your workflow from start to finish.

Lightning-Fast Retrieval

Retrieve any reel in under 4 seconds. Our automated system eliminates manual searching, drastically reducing kitting time and machine downtime.

Real-Time Inventory Control

With integrated barcode/RFID scanning and intelligent sensors, your inventory is updated instantly, providing 100% accuracy and full traceability.

Multi-color LEDs illuminate the exact reel location, guiding operators for error-free picking, even with simultaneous multi-kitting tasks.
