Flexible for Any Production Environment

Whether you're running high-volume or high-mix assembly, our system adapts to your needs.

Maximize Uptime

For high-volume lines, minimizing downtime is critical. Our system ensures a constant, rapid flow of materials to your pick-and-place machines, preventing starvation and maximizing OEE. Automated replenishment alerts keep you ahead of shortages.

Accelerate Changeovers

High-mix, low-volume environments demand agility. Drastically reduce changeover times by kitting entire jobs in minutes instead of hours. The system's accuracy ensures the correct parts for each unique build, every time.

Ensure Full Compliance

For industries like medical or automotive, complete traceability is non-negotiable. Our system logs every transaction, linking each unique reel ID to a specific work order, providing an unbreakable audit trail from storage to assembly.
