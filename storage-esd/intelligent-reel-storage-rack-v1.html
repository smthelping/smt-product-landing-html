Simple & Efficient Operation

1. Material Inbound

Scan the reel and an available location. The LED indicator lights up, guiding you to place the reel. The system automatically records the location.

2. Material Outbound

A work order from your MES/ERP triggers the system. The tower light indicates the correct rack, and LEDs pinpoint the exact reels to be picked.

3. Line-Side Replenishment

Picked materials are placed on a mobile trolley. Scan the material and the feeder at the production line for a final error-proof check.
