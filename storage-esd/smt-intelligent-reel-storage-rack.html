THE PROBLEM

Inventory Chaos & Costly Mispicks

Manual tracking leads to inaccurate stock levels, misplaced reels, and wrong parts being sent to the line. Each error means downtime, rework, and wasted materials—chipping away at your bottom line.

THE SOLUTION

Achieve Flawless Accuracy

Our system provides absolute control. Every reel gets a unique ID, and sensors track every movement. LED Pick-to-Light technology guides operators to the exact part, every time.

  • 100% Real-Time Inventory Visibility
  • Eliminate Human Picking Errors
  • Full Traceability for Quality Control

A Simple, Powerful Workflow

Transform your material handling in four streamlined steps.

1

Scan & Store

Scan the reel's barcode. The system assigns a unique ID and LEDs guide you to an open slot.

2

Import Job

Import your production list from your MES or ERP. The system verifies component availability instantly.

3

Pick with Light

LEDs light up for every part in the kitting list. Pick with 100% accuracy in seconds.

4

Automate & Analyze

The system automatically updates inventory and provides data for analysis and process optimization.
